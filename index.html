Personal information we handle

Information depends on how you use our services:

  • Account: name, email, phone, account identifiers, and sign-in information. Google sign-in, when selected, provides the details allowed by its permissions screen, such as your name, email, and profile image. We do not ask for your Google password.

  • Support: messages, preferred language, issue details, responses, and files where uploads are enabled. Redact passwords, tokens, unrelated personal information, and payment credentials from screenshots.
  • Technical records: IP address, device/browser information, requests, error logs, session details, and security events.

This legal-centre page has no advertising, analytics, third-party fonts, or application cookies of its own. Its hosting/network provider may process request logs. Other CodingMaxima services and external sign-in/payment pages may use the storage and services described below.

Refunds or other appropriate remedies remain available for verified duplicate charges, non-delivery (including inability to activate purchased access), deficient services, material misdescription, and any other legal entitlement. Starting a lesson does not extinguish these rights. We will not classify a material teaching failure as a harmless typo to refuse a remedy.

POLICY 10

Content quality, accessibility & corrections

Our teaching uses extensive text, code, diagrams, interview explanations, animations, and debugging steps. Despite review, a lesson may have a typo, missing step, inaccurate label, wrong output, outdated dependency, or an animation that differs from the intended explanation. Debugging lessons may deliberately show a failing example before a correction; that context should be clear.

Animations are conceptual aids. Timing, memory layout, thread ordering, network activity, and movement may be simplified, not precise measurements or live runtime traces. Results vary by version, operating system, configuration, and input. See the example-code terms for safe practice expectations.

Report an error with its course, lesson URL, section, code/animation step, expected and actual behaviour, and relevant software version. We review reports, prioritise material correctness and safety, and correct or clarify as appropriate. Complex fixes require investigation; not every report is resolved immediately. Honest reviews and lawful remedies remain available.

If motion, flashing, text size, contrast, keyboard interaction, or assistive-technology compatibility prevents learning, tell us the affected page and required format so we can assess a practical alternative. This legal centre respects reduced-motion preferences and supports keyboard navigation and printing.
